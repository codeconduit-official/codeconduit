From idea to working

A practical path, scoped around your project.

  1. Understand the work

    Walk through the task, the people involved, and the tools you use. Define what a useful outcome looks like.

  2. Shape the solution

    Turn requirements into a scope. Identify the integrations, constraints, and decisions that matter before building.

  3. Build and refine

    Build around real use cases. Review it against your workflow and refine the details.

  4. Put it to work

    Deployment, documentation, and handover, plus ongoing support when you need it.

05

Questions

Do I need a fully formed idea?

No. Start with the task that takes too long, the tools that do not connect, or the software you wish existed. That is enough to begin.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. Integrations are a core part of what we do. What is possible depends on the tools, API access, permissions, and technical constraints involved.

Can AI agents use our systems without exposing client data?

Yes, with the right design. We build access layers, such as custom MCP servers with privacy filters, that give agents what they need and nothing more.

How much will a project cost?

Custom work needs a defined scope. Share what you want to achieve, your current setup, and any timing or budget constraints, and we will propose an approach.
