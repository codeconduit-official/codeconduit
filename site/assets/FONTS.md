# Typography

Panchang and Switzer are by the Indian Type Foundry, provided through Fontshare for personal and commercial use. This site loads their webfonts directly from Fontshare's CDN; it does not redistribute the font files in its repository.

- https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/panchang
- https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer
- https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl

Fontshare receives the browser's normal font requests. Arial and system sans-serif are the fallback fonts.
